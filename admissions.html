Application Process

How to Apply

Follow these five simple steps to complete your application.

1
Register Online

Create an account on the COMSATS admissions portal at admissions.comsats.edu.pk

2
Fill Application

Complete the online form with your personal, academic, and program details.

3
Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of transcripts, CNIC, passport photos, and test scores.

4
Pay Fee

Submit the non-refundable application processing fee of PKR 2,000 via bank or online.

5
Await Merit List

Monitor the admissions portal for your merit list result and enrollment instructions.

Eligibility

Admission Criteria

Minimum requirements for each degree program.

🎯 BSCS — Undergraduate

  • Intermediate (FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS) or A-Levels
  • Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate
  • NTS / SAT / COMSATS entry test score required
  • Mathematics as a compulsory subject
  • Age limit: 22 years at time of admission
  • Pakistani nationals and foreign students both eligible
  • Commerce / Arts background not eligible

🧠 MSCS — Graduate

  • 16-year education (BS / BCS / BE in CS or related)
  • Minimum CGPA 2.0 / 60% marks in Bachelor's
  • GAT General (NTS) score of 50+ required
  • GRE Subject score accepted in lieu of GAT
  • Interview may be required for shortlisted candidates
  • Working professionals encouraged to apply
  • Evening programme available for employed applicants

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Is there an entry test? +
Yes. BSCS applicants must appear in the COMSATS entry test or submit a valid NTS / SAT score. The entry test covers Mathematics, English, and basic Computer Science concepts. Test dates are published on the admissions portal.
Can I apply with ICS (Physics)? +
Yes. ICS with Physics or Mathematics is accepted for BSCS admission. You must have at least 50% marks overall and Mathematics must be one of your subjects.
When is the next intake? +
The next intake is Fall 2025, with classes beginning September 1, 2025. The application deadline is June 15, 2025. A Spring 2026 intake is also planned for BSCS only.
Can I transfer from another university? +
Credit transfer is possible for students from HEC-recognized institutions. A minimum CGPA of 2.5 is required and courses are evaluated individually by the academic committee. Transfer applications must be submitted before the regular deadline.
Is evening / weekend programme available? +
Yes. The MSCS programme offers an evening track specifically designed for working professionals. Classes are held on weekday evenings and Saturday mornings. This track has the same curriculum and degree as the regular programme.
How do I apply for a scholarship? +
Merit scholarships are awarded automatically based on your entry test score. For need-based and other scholarships, submit the scholarship application form along with supporting documents to the Scholarships Office within two weeks of enrollment.
